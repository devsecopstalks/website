# DevSecOps Talks — writing style (articles & podcast copy)

Episode articles follow [`blog-tone-of-voice.md`](blog-tone-of-voice.md) for
voice, framing and structure. This guide covers everything else generated from
an episode (titles, teasers, show notes, YouTube copy) and its phrasing and
accuracy rules apply to articles too.

## Show identity

- **Podcast:** DevSecOps Talks (`devsecops.fm`).
- **Hosts:** Andrey Devyatkin, Paulina Dubas and Mattias Hemmingsson. Use full
  names on first mention in third-person copy, first names after that.
- **Audience:** DevSecOps practitioners — engineers and leaders who ship and secure software; assume curiosity and skepticism, not beginners-only fluff.

## Tone

- **Plain language:** short sentences, concrete examples. Prefer clarity over rhetorical flourish.
- **Short dashes only.** No em dashes on any surface.
- **Avoid** filler praise and hype adjectives. Do **not** use words such as: **delve**, **engaging**, **enlightening**, “warmly welcome”, “thrilled to announce”, “game-changing”, “leverage”, or similar stock-podcast phrasing.
- **No empty gravitas:** "The stakes couldn't be higher", "This matters more than ever". Say what is at risk and for whom, or cut the sentence.
- **No unbacked absolutes:** "always", "never", "zero" need a source or a quote.

## Titles

- **Hook first, number later.** Never include the episode number; the pipeline adds `#NN -`.
- **Under 55 characters** where possible, with the most recognizable term or problem in the first three words.
- **Guests are named.** When there is a guest, every title includes their full name, even if that pushes it past 55 characters.
- **Outcome over label:** "Stop Renaming Repos Into Broken Deploys" beats "GitHub OIDC Changes".
- Proven shapes: problem/solution, versus, question, number/list.

## Teaser (description)

The picked teaser is the page standfirst, the meta description and the list-page
summary, so it must stand alone:

- Plain text, two or three sentences, no Markdown.
- Open with the problem in the reader's terms; the first 200 characters say why it matters.
- Name every guest by full name.
- Prefer a question over a flat statement where it fits.

## Podcast show notes and YouTube copy

Rules live in [`podcast-description.md`](podcast-description.md) and
[`youtube-description.md`](youtube-description.md). Both are third person and
must not claim anything the episode did not say.

## Cross-checks

- Names of hosts, guests, companies, and tools must match **`podcast-context.md`**, the verified guest context, and verified references — web-search URLs before asserting versions or GA dates.
